[01] — about

A geophysicist who codes the inverse of the Earth.

I'm Minh Nhat Tran — a PhD student in Geophysics at the University of Houston, working with Dr. Jiaxuan Li. My research sits at the intersection of seismology, numerical methods, and high-performance computing.

I work primarily with Full Waveform Inversion (FWI), Distributed Acoustic Sensing (DAS), and wave-propagation modeling — using physics and optimization to reconstruct subsurface images of the Earth.

Before geophysics, I trained as a civil engineer at Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology, then spent nearly two years as a research assistant at the University of Florida. Each step has taught me a different way to read the ground beneath us.

[03] — research

What I'm working on.

◐

Full Waveform Inversion

Reconstructing high-resolution subsurface velocity models by minimizing the misfit between observed and simulated waveforms — an inverse problem as much in optimization as in physics.

≋

Distributed Acoustic Sensing

Turning fiber-optic cables into dense seismic arrays. Processing DAS signals and adapting classical methods for a fundamentally new kind of geophysical sensor.

∿

Wave-Propagation Modeling

Numerical simulation of elastic and acoustic waves through complex geological media — the forward problem that every inversion depends on.

⌬

HPC & Optimization

Designing and scaling numerical workflows on high-performance computing clusters — because realistic geophysical problems do not fit on a laptop.
